Foundation

After some huge arguments with the members of their team at the time,Juventus, the swissman Alfrid Dick and some of his players left the club to join some of the players from another club,FC Torinese. In December 3 1906 they've founded a new team: Torino FC

Golden years

During the 40s, Torino were a unstoppable winning machine:
The club won the italian league 9 consecutive times (1942/43, 1945/46, 1946/47, 1947/48 e 1948/49)
they also won an italian cup.

Superga Tragedy

Unfortunelly, in may 9 1949 their plane crashed againist the Basilica of Superga.Everyone in there passed away.

The football world was devasted with that tragedy and clubs worldwide paid tribute
to the team

Recent Years

Torino,after their golden era, still reached good achievments,including 3 italian cups (1967-68,1970-71,1992-93),
1 italian league (1976) and a UEFA cup final (the modern Europa League) in 1991 which they had been beaten by Ajax.

The club still relevant to this day,ocasionally reaching the Europa League acess, but majority
a mid-table team.
